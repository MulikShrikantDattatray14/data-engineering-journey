# Daily Study Schedule — Six-Month Data Engineering Transition

**Adopted:** 2026-11-06 (Asia/Kolkata)
**Commitment:** 4 focused hours daily, 6 days weekly; one flexible rest/review day.
**Planned:** 24 hours per week, approximately 624 hours over 26 weeks.

| Block | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Easy-language theory and worked examples | 60 minutes |
| 2 | Independent hands-on coding practice | 120 minutes |
| 3 | Debugging, active recall and mixed-concept revision | 30 minutes |
| 4 | GitHub notes, learning journal and revision index | 30 minutes |

## Our learning rules
- Explain what, why, when and how using simple language and realistic scenarios.
- Progress from easy problems to medium and advanced multi-concept questions.
- Ask the learner to attempt each task before revealing answers.
- Give hints, review edge cases, and discuss performance after correctness.
- Keep initial attempts and reviewed solutions separate.
- Commit reviewed work only, with honest completion tracking.
- Reassess pace each week; protect sleep, work and recovery.
